## 🚀 About Me

I am a builder. I like taking an idea, figuring out what it needs to become real, and then actually shipping it.

Right now, most of my time goes into **Bontro**, a zero-commission booking and payments platform for independent service professionals. I built it across web, iOS, Android, and in-shop hardware.

Before I started building software, I spent six years cutting hair. Bontro came from problems I dealt with myself, like booking through DMs, chasing payments, losing track of clients, and using platforms that take too much from the people doing the actual work.

I do more than write code. I think through the product, talk to users, decide what should be built, and figure out how to get it in front of people.

Outside of that, I am usually playing basketball, writing poetry, taking pictures, or helping younger students find their way through computer science.

* Includes a no-code website builder for professionals who do not already have a website.
* Available across iOS, Android, and the web.
